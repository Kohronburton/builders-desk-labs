# Catering Assembly SOP

## Staging

Separate cold items, hot mains, finishing ingredients, disposables, and service equipment before assembly begins.

## Batch control

Build plated components in batches of 20. Label every completed batch with item name, quantity, allergen flag, destination, and completion time.

## Temperature control

Keep hot foods above 140°F and chilled foods below 41°F. Record holding checks before loading.

## Final quality assurance

Match packed quantities against the approved production count. Confirm serving utensils, labels, backup portions, and vehicle loading order before release.
